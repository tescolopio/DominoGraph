# Domino framework

Assess every economic node along independent dimensions:

| Dimension | Question | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pressure | What underlying stress affects the node? | Delinquencies, shortages, refinancing stress |
| Firebreak | What natural buffers reduce transmission? | Equity, capital, diversification, inventories |
| Support | What deliberate stabilization exists? | Facilities, guarantees, subsidies, regulatory relief |
| Dependency | How reliant is the node on continued support? | Financing or production dependent on intervention |
| Transmission | Where and how could stress move? | Credit losses, input costs, demand contraction |

Expose confidence separately. A strong firebreak is not pressure, and absence of
support is not automatically stress. Measurements and policy statements remain
distinct. A rising value can represent stress, a buffer, or neither depending on
the metric's documented contextual direction.

Initial scores are transparent illustrative indices, not calibrated probabilities.
Avoid composites until the separate dimensions have passed empirical validation.
